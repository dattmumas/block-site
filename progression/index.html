A white lamp is a pass Pro

Each set with a planned load or reps gets three lamps. One red lamp makes the set a no lift, and the set screen names the reason.

  • Good liftAll reps, full load, and RPE at or under the lift's target RPE.
  • No lift on repsShort of the target reps: the set's reps, or the bottom of its range.
  • No lift on loadUnder the planned load.
  • No lift on effortRPE over the lift's target RPE.
  • Not judgedWarm-ups, drop sets, prep lifts, and sets with no planned load or reps.

A weight-and-reps set that beats the target's estimated max passes reps and load together: 360 × 8 against a target of 365 × 6 is the stronger set. The estimated max is the load × (1 + reps / 30).

Each lift has a target RPE, 8 when you set none. The set screen logs each set at the target RPE. Tap another number when the set was easier or harder. No RPE counts as a pass.

For an assisted lift, less help is the harder lift. The load lamp is white at the planned help or less.

The trainer sets the next targets ProiOS 27

After you save a session, the app asks the AI trainer for the workout's next targets. The trainer reads each set: the load, the reps, the RPE and the lamps. It also reads your notes on the lift, the rep range and the week of the block.

How the trainer sets each lift's next load and reps
1A good lift: all reps, full load, and RPE at or under the target RPE.
2Every set a good lift at the top of its range: more load, and the reps start again at the bottom of the range.The lift's step: 10 lb or 5 kg for a big lower-body lift, 5 lb or 2.5 kg for any other lift. More when your note says so.+10
3A set under the top of the range: more reps at the same load, or more load when your sets show you are ready.Reps +1
4A hard session (RPE over the target, a red lamp), pain, or work where more is not the point: the lift can hold, and the line says why. A whole workout can hold.Hold
5After a session of good lifts, the load does not go down. A lower load comes only after misses, for a deload, or when you ask, and the line says why.Same
6Deload week: Today marks it, and the trainer sets its lighter numbers for that session only.Deload

A note on the lift that names the next step comes first. With “Felt light, can step 20 next time”, the next load is 20 more.

Approve each line

The trainer's answer shows one line per lift: the numbers now, the new numbers and the reason. Approve saves a line to the workout. Reject leaves the lift as it is. Approve all and Reject all decide the rest. A hold needs no decision, and nothing changes until you approve.

The app checks each line. A line that lowers a load after a session of good lifts, or keeps a load after every set reached the top of its range, goes back to the trainer, unless the trainer gives the reason.

A lift with a set logged in the open session is done for today. The trainer does not change it. Its next targets come after you save the session.

Not approved, not changed

The app does not change a load or reps by itself. With Block Pro, until you approve new targets, the next session starts at the numbers you last lifted, and Today says: No new targets from the trainer. Without Pro, it starts at the workout's numbers.

No new targets when

  • The iPhone has no connection, or the trainer had an error.
  • The month's messages are used up, or you have not allowed Claude yet.
  • The iPhone is on iOS 26 or earlier.

Tap Ask trainer on Today to ask again. You can change a workout at any time in the workout editor, or with a connected assistant such as Claude.

Loads your gym can make

The trainer uses only loads your plates can make. Today shows each lift's last result and a tag for the change: +10, Reps +1, Same or New.
