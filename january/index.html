Training blocks and the AI trainer are part of Block Pro: $4.99 a month, or $39.99 a year with a two-week free trial for new subscribers. The trainer needs iOS 27.

Set it up in December

Set up the block before the new year. On Monday, January 4, Today shows the first session.

  1. Open Programs in Plan > New block or New workout, or in Settings. Programs that fit About you come first.

  2. Pick a program and tap Start this program.

  3. Set First day to Monday, January 4. Name the block, then tap Start block. With Block Pro, the program becomes a training block.

  4. Check the training days in Edit block: a workout or Rest for each day, Monday first. The program's week comes with it.

  5. Lift. Choose each load in your first session. After each session, the AI trainer sets the next loads.

Programs have sets, rep ranges and rest, but no loads. Choose each load in the first session. The next session starts at the loads you lifted, until the trainer sets new ones.

Miss a day, and the session moves to the next free day of that week. Accept the move, or pick another day.

On iOS 27, the AI trainer (Pro) can move the days or swap a lift for you. Tap Adjust with the trainer on the program's page.

Without Pro, a program starts as a folder of workouts. There is no block and no trainer.

The trainer sets the next loads ProiOS 27

After each session, the AI trainer reads every set and proposes each lift's next load and reps. Each line you approve is saved to the workout. It uses only loads your plates can make.

How the trainer sets each lift's next load and reps
1A good lift: all reps, full load, RPE at or under the target RPE.
2Every set a good lift at the top of its range: more load, and the reps start again at the bottom.The lift's step: 10 lb or 5 kg for a big lower-body lift, 5 lb or 2.5 kg for any other lift.+10
3Good lifts under the top of the range: more reps at the same load, or more load when your sets show you are ready.Reps +1
4A hard session, pain, or work where more is not the point: the lift can hold, and the line says why. A whole workout can hold. After good lifts, the load does not go down unless you ask.Hold
5Deload week: Today marks it, and the trainer sets its lighter numbers for that session only.Deload

Nothing approved yet? With Block Pro, the next session starts at the numbers you lifted; without Pro, at the workout's numbers.

Short answers

Do I need Block Pro?

For the training block and the AI trainer, yes. Pro Yearly starts with a two-week free trial for new subscribers. Without Pro, you can still log every session.

Can I start on another day?

Yes. You pick the first day. A block counts its weeks from that day.

Which program fits me?

Fill in About you. It is free. Programs that fit your goal, your level, your days and your time come first.

What if my gym has no small plates?

The trainer uses only loads your plates can make. Tell the Block app your plates in Today > Settings > Your gym.
