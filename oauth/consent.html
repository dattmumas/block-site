Connect an assistant

This page opens from an assistant, when you connect it to Block.

In your assistant, such as Claude, add Block as a connector. Use the address in the app: Today > Settings > AI > Connect an assistant.

Sign in with your Block account to connect your assistant.

Use a password instead

Signed up with Apple? Set a password in the app first, in Today > Settings > AI > Connect an assistant. Then sign in here with the email shown there.

An assistant wants to use your Block account.

It will be able to

  • Read your sessions, workouts, blocks, exercises, body measurements and profile.
  • Log sessions and measurements, and create or change workouts and blocks.
  • Delete a workout or a session when you ask it to.

It will not be able to

  • See your password, make or see API keys, or delete your account.

Connected assistants need Block Pro. What the assistant receives follows its own privacy terms. Disconnect it at any time in the app: Today > Settings > AI > Connected assistants.
